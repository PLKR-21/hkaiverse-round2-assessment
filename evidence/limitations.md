# Limitations and unresolved evidence

This project is a candidate-led proposal and prototype. The limitations below are material and must not be hidden in the final submission.

1. **Curriculum transcription:** for this assignment, the candidate confirms the supplied curriculum is the official baseline. Course codes for current Semester VII subjects were not supplied and remain blank; transcription should be checked against the candidate’s source copy.
2. **Credit baseline:** the supplied official baseline lists 136 credits through Semester VI, 16 current Semester VII credits and a planned 20-credit Semester VIII internship, totalling 172 across completed/current/planned components. This total must not be described as 172 credits already completed.
3. **Semester VII details:** course codes were not provided for the five current subjects; fields remain blank. “Human Computer Vision” is preserved as supplied and needs confirmation against the official title.
4. **Workflow fieldwork:** no current campus workflow observation or voluntary conversation is documented. Lab fault-reporting remains a hypothetical walkthrough unless real permitted evidence is added.
5. **Time-saving figures:** 20/40/60 requests and the 12-to-7 minute handling change plus 90 minutes overhead are illustrative assumptions, not measurements from the institution.
6. **Job-description sample:** three individual employer postings have explicit dates visible in the pages reviewed. The sample is small and purposive; two postings include 2–5 years or 3–5 years experience language, and the Amazon university posting targets 2025/2026 graduates. Do not imply every role is suitable for a 2027 graduate.
7. **AI exercises:** prompts and output excerpts are disclosed as generated in this ChatGPT conversation on 2026-10-23. Candidate review and source checks remain pending; the OS exercise explicitly requires textbook/lecture verification, and code/query execution should be done locally before claiming execution.
8. **Faculty/graduate feedback:** not obtained in this draft. If access is unavailable, state that rather than inventing a response.
9. **Prototype:** Streamlit runs locally and reads local CSV files. It has no authentication, role enforcement, production data, database, or college integration.
10. **Credit impact:** the default proposal maps 43 distinct affected credits out of the supplied 172-credit baseline (25.0%). This is an affected-credit share, not a net credit addition/removal. Confirm the course map and obtain academic approval before implementation.
11. **Employability claim:** the proposal cannot prove that changes improve employment outcomes. Existing assessments, employer feedback and programme constraints could disconfirm the proposed gap analysis.
