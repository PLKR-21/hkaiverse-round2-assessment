## Source basis and baseline decision
For this assignment, the candidate has explicitly confirmed that the supplied course/curriculum record is the official curriculum baseline to use for the 2023 admission cohort. The project therefore treats the supplied course codes, titles, credits/units, semester sequence, and current/planned components as the assignment baseline. The public IFHE/IcfaiTech programme page is retained as institutional context, not as a replacement for the candidate-confirmed cohort record.

The dataset distinguishes completed courses recorded in the supplied grade sheet from Semester VII courses currently in progress and the planned Semester VIII internship. Semester VII codes are left blank where the candidate did not supply them; no codes are invented.

## 20–25% affected-credit calculation
The proposal materially updates learning outcomes or assessed practice across 43 distinct credits, counted once per course/period. Within-course changes do not add or remove credit values; conditional lab/module ideas are embedded or reallocated within the existing baseline by default.

- Distinct affected credits: 43
- Baseline programme credits: 172
- Affected-credit share: 43 / 172 × 100 = **25.0%**
- Baseline credit total after proposal: **172 credits** (no net addition or removal in the default proposal)

The credit map is in `affected-credit-map.csv`. This is an affected-credit share, not a claim that 25% of credits are deleted or newly added. Any later proposal that changes course credit values must be recalculated and approved through the applicable academic process.
